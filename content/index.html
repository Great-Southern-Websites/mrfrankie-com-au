About us

Who is Mr Frankie?

Created for the locals by the locals, Mr Frankie is a family owned business focused on delivering the local community a little slice of Melbourne to their doorstep. With quality sourced produce, top of the range coffee from Industry Beans, locally sourced wine and warm, friendly service, along with our architecturally designed fit-out, we have created a really special atmosphere for the locals to enjoy and be proud to bring their friends from all over.

Our food

For the locals by the locals

At Mr Frankie we source quality produce and support local businesses where possible. We thrive on quality and consistency to deliver the very best every time. We cater to all dietary needs with a wide variety of gluten free and vegan options. Our menu changes seasonally, keeping it fresh and fun, with a variety of specials available daily.
