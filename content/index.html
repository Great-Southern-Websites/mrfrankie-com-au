About Us

Who is Mr Frankie?

Created for the locals by the locals, Mr Frankie is a family owned business with a focus on delivering the local community a little slice of Melbourne to their doorstep. With quality sourced produce, top of the range coffee from Industry Beans, locally sourced wine and warm friendly service, along with our architecturally designed fit out, we have created a really special atmosphere for the locals to enjoy and be proud to bring their friends from all over.

Opened in May 2019, Mr Frankie is a Melbourne inspired cafe transformed from an old newsagency.
