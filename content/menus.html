Our Menus

By Day, By Night and Drinks

Our menu changes seasonally, keeping it fresh and fun, with a variety of specials available daily. We cater to all dietaries with a wide variety of gluten free and vegan options.

  • By Day

    Breakfast and brunch, sides, kids menu and the display fridge. Tuesday to Sunday.

  • By Night

    Tapas, kids plates and after dinner, available from 5.00pm on Mr Frankie by Night evenings.

  • Drinks

    Industry Beans coffee, chai, tea, iced drinks, sparkling, smoothies, milkshakes and juices.
