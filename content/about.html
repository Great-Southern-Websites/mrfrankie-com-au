About Us

Who is Mr Frankie?

Created for the locals by the locals.

Created for the locals by the locals, Mr Frankie is a family owned business with a focus on delivering the local community a little slice of Melbourne to their doorstep. With quality sourced produce, top of the range coffee from Industry Beans, locally sourced wine and warm friendly service, along with our architecturally designed fit out, we have created a really special atmosphere for the locals to enjoy and be proud to bring their friends from all over.

Opened in May 2019 and home to one of the best brunch cafes in the surrounding area, Mr Frankie delivers a Melbourne inspired cafe transformed from an old newsagency into an architectural design fit out with attention to detail. Its soft and warm colour palette, pale pink mosaic tiling and colourful curve edged wall features with bulging wall mounted light globes are appealing to the eye in every area of the space. Customers could feel mistaken for entering an old school '90s LA diner.

Our food

For the locals by the locals

At Mr Frankie we source quality produce and support local businesses where possible. We thrive on quality and consistency to deliver the very best every time. We cater to all dietaries with a wide variety of gluten free and vegan options. Our menu changes seasonally, keeping it fresh and fun, with a variety of specials available daily.

Locally sourced wine, top of the range produce and incredibly delicious coffee: Mr Frankie is a really special atmosphere for the locals to enjoy and feel proud to bring their friends from all over.
